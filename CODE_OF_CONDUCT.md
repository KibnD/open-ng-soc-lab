# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community where people can participate without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or retaliation. We value honest evidence, patient explanation, constructive disagreement, privacy, authorization, and responsible defensive security practice.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome differing experience levels and communicate clearly.
- Respect scope, consent, privacy, licensing, and responsible disclosure.
- Represent results, limitations, and provenance accurately.
- Accept correction, resolve conflict constructively, and protect sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, stalking, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, retaliation, or publishing another person's private information are prohibited. So are credentials, confidential evidence, unsafe offensive material, or instructions encouraging unauthorized access.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation; issue warnings; temporarily restrict participation; or permanently ban a participant, proportionate to context and impact. They will avoid unnecessary disclosure and conflicts of interest.

There is not yet a confidential community-conduct mailbox. For a nonsensitive concern, open a minimal issue without names or private evidence and ask the maintainer to establish a private channel. For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish sensitive details because a private channel is unavailable.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. It is informed by common open-source community standards but is original project text; it does not imply that the repository's pending license has been resolved.
