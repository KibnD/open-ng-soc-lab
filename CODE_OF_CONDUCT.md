# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and critique work rather than people;
- respect authorization, privacy, licensing, and responsible disclosure;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and doxxing;
- avoid sharing credentials, confidential evidence, or unsafe offensive material;
- represent test results and limitations honestly.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. A private enforcement contact will be added before public participation opens.
